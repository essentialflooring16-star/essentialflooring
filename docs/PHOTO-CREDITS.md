# Photo credits

The client's own job photographs live in `src/assets/portfolio/`,
`src/assets/before-after/` and `src/assets/progress/`, and are the only images
ever presented as our work.

Seven stock photographs are used as illustration only (materials, samples,
rooms in general), never captioned as a job of ours. All are from Pexels under
the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free for commercial use,
modification allowed, attribution not required.

**The list is no longer only in this file.** It lives in
`src/data/photo-credits.ts` and is printed on the public
[Terms of Use](https://essentialflooringinc.com/terms-of-use/) page, so a
visitor can see for himself which few pictures are not the client's own work.
Adding a stock photograph to the site means adding a line to that file.

Every photographer name in it was read off the photo page on pexels.com on
9 September 2026, not guessed from a filename. Checked at the same time: none
of the seven shows an identifiable face or a brand, which covers the two
restrictions in the Pexels License that could actually bite.
